Executive Summary

Through October, Quest's paid program has delivered 6.05M impressions and 36.3K clicks (0.60% CTR) at a blended $2.61 CPC / $15.70 CPM. Qualified conversions total 173: 28 StackAdapt ABM, 137 non-brand Search and 8 LinkedIn form leads. Brand and remarketing PMax added another 724 low-cost conversions. October was the biggest reach month of the year (905K impressions) as the Q4 flights launched at half the CPM. The gap is still the same one: clicks aren't turning into conversions.

  • Top win: cheaper reach and clicks in Q4. StackAdapt Q4 flights are running at a $4.89 CPM (vs ~$9.80 all year) and a $2.20 CPC. LinkedIn's Est. 4782 website-visit ad sets hit 2.3–2.7% CTR at $0.80–$1.12 CPC, the cheapest LinkedIn clicks this year.
  • Watch-out: Google Search pulled back in October. Google impressions fell 56% MoM to 4.6K. Search CPC rose to $13.23 (vs $7.19 YTD) and October Search CPA was $221 on 7 conversions. Accuracy – KZ spent $418 in October with no conversions.
  • Strategic note: fix the post-click step. The -kazoo landing pages drew 4.2K more sessions in October but recorded zero new key events (16 all year from 25.9K sessions). A landing-page and key-event audit is the highest-leverage Q4 move.

Q4 halves the cost of reach

The $5 CPM bid is delivering: Q4 flights averaged a $4.89 CPM and $2.20 CPC in October, the cheapest clicks of any StackAdapt quarter. Pacing is on track at about 724K impressions in month one.

Conversions are still stuck

Q4 produced 1 conversion in October (Accuracy). Q2, Q3 and Q4-to-date have each produced 1–4 conversions per quarter, against 16 in Q1. More clicks haven't moved conversions.

Q4 CTR slipped back from Q3

Q4 CTR is 0.22%, back to Q1/Q2 levels after Q3's 0.36%. Cheaper inventory may be lower quality. Worth watching whether the CPC savings hold if CTR keeps sliding.

Q1 still the conversion benchmark

PCI Q1 ($580) and Accuracy Q1 ($581) remain the most efficient ABM flights to date. A Becker's Healthcare geofence campaign is in draft.

Provider Network Management is the Search anchor

Lowest non-brand CPA ($166) and the most conversions (45, +3 in October). Payers & Plans converts at the highest rate (6.81%), so its $13.43 CPC still yields a $197 CPA.

October Search volume dropped and CPCs doubled

Search delivered 1,892 impressions and 117 clicks in October at a $13.23 CPC (vs $7.19 YTD). Search impression share is under 10% on every campaign. Bids and budgets need a look.

Accuracy CPA is drifting up

Accuracy spent its October budget with no conversions, pushing its CPA to $236 (from $220). It still has the best CTR (4.41%) but converts at 1.93%, half the Search average.

Brand and remarketing remain efficient

PMax brand/remarketing converts at $5–6 CPA. Quest Brand is "Limited by budget". Its conversions are mostly engagement events, so they're reported separately from qualified Search conversions.

Paid landing pages aren't holding visitors

The -kazoo pages drew 25.9K sessions (9.2% of site traffic) with a 3-second average engagement and just 16 key events, all on /kazoo and /contact-kazoo. They added 4.2K sessions in October with no new key events.

Paid traffic is under-attributed

GA4 credits only 638 Paid Search sessions against 11.9K Google clicks, and Display just 1,600. Most paid visits land in Direct (55% of sessions). A UTM and auto-tagging audit would sharpen channel reporting.

Referral and Organic carry conversion quality

Referral converts at 5.2% key events per session (about 5× the site average). Organic Search holds a 52% engagement rate across 98K sessions.
